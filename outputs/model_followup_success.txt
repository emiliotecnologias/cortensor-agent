openminion: Samsung's Galaxy A-series phones are commonly found with issues of not charging. The most frequent cause is a worn or damaged USB cable, while lint packed into the charging port prevents it from seating fully. Faulty charging boards (sub-boards) can also break the charging path between the port and the main board. Swollen or dead batteries prevent them from accepting charge even when connected to a charger.
First diagnostic step: Check the quality of the USB cable, ensuring it's not worn out. Next, inspect the charging port for any lint buildup and ensure the charging board is functioning correctly by testing multiple chargers on different devices. If these steps do not resolve the issue, further investigation into battery health may be necessary.
